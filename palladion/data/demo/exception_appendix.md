## 1. Delayed settlement acknowledgement

[CLAUSE: EX-SETTLEMENT-DELAY]
If the settlement service reports PENDING or times out, support must not infer UNSETTLED. Keep the case in investigation, record the source and observation time, and request a settlement recheck. This exception clarifies the missing-status rule and does not waive the settlement prerequisite.

## 2. Explicit exception boundary

[CLAUSE: EX-NO-WAIVER]
This appendix does not permit reversal when settlement is unknown, pending, or settled. It does not supersede the transaction-status requirement in the applicable SOP.
