# DEMO.DISPUTE.SOP — Revision 2025.1

Fictional training material. Clause markers are stable fixture identifiers. Approval and effective dates are registry metadata, not claims made by this document.

## 1. Scope

[CLAUSE: S25-SCOPE]
This procedure applies to QR_DEMO transactions in the DEMO_STANDARD class for the historical effective period recorded by the source registry.

## 2. Investigate a reported debit

[CLAUSE: S25-INVESTIGATE]
When a customer reports a debit but the merchant has not confirmed receipt, support must check transaction and settlement status before recommending a reversal. A failed transaction record alone does not establish that settlement did not occur.

## 3. Historical reversal review

[CLAUSE: S25-REVERSAL]
For a QR_DEMO DEMO_STANDARD transaction that occurred before 2026-07-01, a reversal review may proceed only when the authoritative settlement record is UNSETTLED and the transaction record is FAILED. If either required status is missing, keep the case open and request the missing status. This procedure does not authorize an automatic payment mutation.

## 4. No settlement result

[CLAUSE: S25-MISSING-SETTLEMENT]
A timeout, missing settlement result, or system-specific not-found response is not proof that settlement did not happen. Describe only the status of the system that was queried and continue investigation.
